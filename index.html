Waivers & FAB

FormatFAB · Rolling List Tiebreak
Season Budget$100
Claims OpenKickoff, Week's 1st Game
Claim Deadline11:59 PM PT Tue
Waivers ProcessedWednesday morning

Every manager gets a $100 budget to bid on unrostered players. High bid wins the player; ties are broken by a continual rolling list.

Trading

Trade DeadlineNov 28, 2026
Review Window2 days
Veto Threshold4 managers (1/3)

Every trade is open to league review for two days after it's made. It only gets vetoed if at least four managers vote against it.

Round-two matchups reseed after any first-round upset, so the highest remaining seed always plays the lowest remaining seed.
