Week 2 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

Three teams are unbeaten after two weeks: Draft Punk, Lebowski Bowling Team, and Circle The Wagons all moved to 2-0. Draft Punk got past Brian's Nifty Team 145.78-126.48, Lebowski Bowling Team held off In Vrabel we trust 123.42-103.36, and Circle The Wagons ran past Mayehem 124.90-86.22.

The high score race, though, came down to the wire — Draft Punk's 145.78 held up by exactly 1.04 points over Yinzers, who put up 144.74 in a losing effort to Whiskey Tango Foxtrot. A photo finish for the trophy, if not for the win column.

On the other side, Mr. Jackson if you're nasty handled Angry Amish 120.40-83.22 for their first win, while Hold the Mayo edged Like a Peacock 117.68-96.98. Like a Peacock, Angry Amish, and Mayehem all fall to 0-2.
