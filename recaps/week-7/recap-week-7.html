Week 7

Week 7 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🥃 Week 7 belonged to Whiskey Tango Foxtrot. Alan put up 147.22, the top score of the week, in a 147.22-96.58 win over Mr. Jackson if you're nasty. It's Whiskey Tango Foxtrot's first week on top this season, and it moves them back above .500 at 4-3. Nice week, Alan.

In Vrabel we trust had the second-best score of the week, beating Like a Peacock 144.92-100.60 to get back in the win column at 5-2. Well played, Adam.

In the big one at the top, Circle The Wagons beat Lebowski Bowling Team 137.16-118.30. That's four straight wins for Circle The Wagons, who now sit alone in 1st at 6-1. Draft Punk held off Yinzers 105.52-96.32 for a second straight win and still leads the league in total points (991.80). Nice win, Matt.

In the closest game of the week, Brian's Nifty Team got past Hold the Mayo 129.90-127.48, a 2.42-point margin, for a second straight win. Nice win, Brian. And Mayehem picked up a 127.64-95.10 win over Angry Amish. Well played, Amy.
