Week 5

Week 5 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🦬 Week 5 belonged to Circle The Wagons. Jayson put up 189.94, the top score of the week and the second-highest of the season so far, in a 189.94-79.90 win over Brian's Nifty Team. That's two straight wins and a move into 1st place. Nice week, Jayson.

In Vrabel we trust made it three wins in a row, beating Angry Amish 150.14-125.80. Lebowski Bowling Team kept pace with a 131.02-78.44 win over Yinzers, so there are now three teams tied at 4-1.

Mayehem had the second-best score of the week, beating Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 158.06-126.76. Well played, Amy. In the closest game of the week, Like a Peacock beat Draft Punk 149.74-136.74. Nice win, Tom.

Whiskey Tango Foxtrot rounded things out with a 123.24-101.00 win over Hold the Mayo to move to 3-2.
