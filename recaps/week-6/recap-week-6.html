Week 6

Week 6 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🏆 Week 6 belonged to Yinzers. Christina put up 146.26, the top score of the week, in a 146.26-105.60 win over Mr. Jackson if you're nasty. It's the defending champ's first week on top this season, and it snaps a two-game skid to get back to .500. Nice week, Christina.

Draft Punk had the second-best score of the week, beating Mayehem 142.86-97.80. That gets Draft Punk back in the win column and keeps it atop the league in total points (886.28).

At the top of the table, Lebowski Bowling Team beat Whiskey Tango Foxtrot 140.18-81.72 and Circle The Wagons beat Hold the Mayo 133.14-97.82. Both are now 5-1 on three-game win streaks, with Lebowski ahead by about three points.

Brian's Nifty Team picked up a big win over In Vrabel we trust, 126.52-81.72, to snap a four-game losing streak. Well played, Brian. And in the closest game of the week, Angry Amish got past Like a Peacock 102.90-95.18. Nice win, Robert.
