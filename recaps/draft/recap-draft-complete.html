Draft Day

The Draft is Complete!

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

Twelve rounds, twelve rosters, and a good amount of beer and wings means that the 2026 draft is officially in the books. No pick-by-pick breakdown in the recap, no reach-of-the-night awards, nobody's getting called out for their kicker in the eighth round..... Just a genuine thank you.

Getting all twelve of us in the same room instead of scattered across a group chat isn't easy to pull off. Good food, better drinks, and some blue cheesed shorts — that's a draft night worth repeating, regardless of how the rosters actually turned out.

The board, fully loaded — sticky notes and all for the picks that came in faster than the sheet could keep up.

If BDubs is still running their draft party setup next year, let's make one small change: everybody still drafts online like normal, but after your pick locks in, you walk up and stick it on the board yourself. That way we can get a league photo and all that jazz at the end. Plus, it was a pain to manually do it after the fact.

The season officially kicks off Wednesday, September 9th. Good luck to all, and we'll find out fast enough who the real peacock is.
