Week 9

Week 9 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🛳️ A quick note: I was out of town on a cruise during Week 9, so this recap is coming out a little late. Week 10 results will follow shortly.

🦬 Week 9 belonged to Circle The Wagons. Jayson put up 164.08, the top score of the week, in a 164.08-156.46 win over Draft Punk. It's Circle The Wagons' second week on top this season (after Week 5), and it came in the 1st-vs-2nd matchup of the week. Nice week, Jayson.

Draft Punk had the second-best score of the week at 156.46, which would have beaten any other team in Week 9. That game was also the closest of the week, decided by 7.62 points, and the two teams combined for 320.54. With the win, Circle The Wagons extends its winning streak to six and moves to 8-1.

Lebowski Bowling Team beat Brian's Nifty Team 132.06-98.38 to snap a two-game skid. Well played, Sean. Whiskey Tango Foxtrot beat Angry Amish 127.10-113.26 for a third straight win. Nice win, Alan. Both teams move to 6-3.

Yinzers beat In Vrabel we trust 129.50-109.70, Like a Peacock beat Mayehem 118.68-106.36 to snap a three-game skid, and Hold the Mayo beat Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 110.14-84.98 for a second straight win. Nice wins, Christina, Tom and Alex.

On to Week 10.
