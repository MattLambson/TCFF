Week 11

Week 11 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🖤💛 Week 11 belonged to Yinzers. Christina put up 134.38, the top score of the week, in a 134.38-110.96 win over Mayehem. It's the defending champ's second week on top this season, after Week 6, and it moves Yinzers up to 7th at 5-6. Nice week, Christina.

Two teams tied for the second-best score of the week at 132.74. Circle The Wagons beat In Vrabel we trust 132.74-113.06 for an eighth straight win and moves to 10-1. Well played, Jayson. Lebowski Bowling Team took the week's marquee matchup, beating Draft Punk 132.74-125.70 for a third straight win. At 8-3, Lebowski Bowling Team joins Circle The Wagons with a playoff spot clinched. Nice win, Sean.

The closest game of the week was Like a Peacock over Whiskey Tango Foxtrot, 130.26-124.30, decided by 5.96 points. That's a third straight win for Like a Peacock, and it ends Whiskey Tango Foxtrot's four-game win streak. Well played, Tom. Brian's Nifty Team beat Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 131.38-90.50 for a second straight win and moves to 6-5. Nice win, Brian. Angry Amish beat Hold the Mayo 105.36-83.40 to get back in the win column. Nice win, Robert.
