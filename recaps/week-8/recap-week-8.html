Week 8

Week 8 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🐴 Week 8 belonged to Angry Amish. Robert put up 156.96, the top score of the week, in a 156.96-131.96 win over Yinzers. It's Angry Amish's first week on top this season, and they got there by just 0.16 points over Brian's Nifty Team. Nice week, Robert.

Brian's Nifty Team had the second-best score of the week, beating Like a Peacock 156.80-134.34. That's three straight wins for Brian's Nifty Team, who are back to .500 at 4-4. Well played, Brian. Mayehem had the third-best score, beating In Vrabel we trust 140.76-124.94 for a second straight win. Nice win, Amy.

Among the leaders, Circle The Wagons beat Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 139.80-55.50 for a fifth straight win. At 7-1, Circle The Wagons is now two games clear in 1st and has passed Draft Punk for the league lead in total points (1,119.80). Whiskey Tango Foxtrot beat Draft Punk 109.40-97.58 for a second straight win. Well played, Alan.

In the closest game of the week, Hold the Mayo beat Lebowski Bowling Team 120.18-111.08, a 9.10-point margin, to snap a three-game skid. Nice win, Alex.
