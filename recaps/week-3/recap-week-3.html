Week 3

Week 3 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🧡💙 D-FENSE! D-FENSE! D-FENSE! 🧡💙 A quick chant and a big thank you to the Broncos defense — I went to bed thinking I'd lost the top score, and woke up very happy about that pick-six. Take a bow, boys.

Draft Punk stays unbeaten at 3-0, cruising past Angry Amish 159.08-72.56. Angry Amish moves to 0-3 and will be looking to get in the win column soon.

Elsewhere, the wins were lopsided. Whiskey Tango Foxtrot ran past Brian's Nifty Team 156.94-117.40, and Yinzers handled Circle The Wagons 146.20-110.88 to climb to 2-1. Both Brian's Nifty Team and Circle The Wagons take a step back after strong starts.

The nail-biters went the other way. Mayehem snuck past Hold the Mayo by less than a point, 129.94-129.18, and Like a Peacock edged Lebowski Bowling Team 123.34-121.50 — Lebowski's first loss of the season. In Vrabel we trust rounded out the week, beating Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 117.42-77.74.
