Week 1 Results Are In 🏆

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

Draft Punk opened the season with a statement, dropping 199.90 to run away with the week's high score — nearly double the total put up by Mr. Jackson if you're nasty, who took the week's toughest loss at 100.26.

The other five winners kept it tighter. Brian's Nifty Team rolled past Yinzers 185.46-111.36, Lebowski Bowling Team edged Angry Amish 152.16-117.46, and In Vrabel we trust held off Hold the Mayo 142.02-124.06. Circle The Wagons beat Like a Peacock 140.70-115.80, and the week's closest game went to Whiskey Tango Foxtrot, who scraped past Mayehem 107.70-98.32.
