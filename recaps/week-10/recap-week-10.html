Week 10

Week 10 Results Are In

2026 Season · Trinity Creek League

🤖 Week 10 belonged to Draft Punk. Matt put up 215.06, the top score of the week, in a 215.06-108.66 win over Hold the Mayo. It's the highest score of the season so far and the first to clear 200, topping Draft Punk's own 199.90 from Week 1. It also moves Draft Punk to the top of the league in points for at 1,460.90. Nice week, Matt.

Whiskey Tango Foxtrot had the second-best score of the week at 186.34 in a win over In Vrabel we trust, a fourth straight win. Nice win, Alan. Circle The Wagons beat Angry Amish 150.58-128.64 for a seventh straight win and moves to 9-1, becoming the first team to clinch a playoff spot. Well played, Jayson. Lebowski Bowling Team beat Mr. Jackson if you're nasty 112.56-106.66 for a second straight win. Nice win, Sean.

The closest game of the week was Brian's Nifty Team over Mayehem, 126.34-125.02, decided by 1.32 points. Well played, Brian. Like a Peacock beat Yinzers 140.52-121.08 for a second straight win. Nice win, Tom.
